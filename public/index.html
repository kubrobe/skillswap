How it works

Three steps to your first swap

No fees and no tutors to pay. Everyone teaches, everyone learns.

  1. Add your skills

    List what you can teach (Python, guitar, plov…) and what you want to learn.

  2. Get matched

    We compare both lists. Fair two-way swaps, where you teach each other, come first.

  3. Swap and grow

    Chat, plan milestones in a shared workspace, meet, and rate each other afterwards.

Features

Everything a swap needs

Built for students in Uzbekistan, shared between this website and the Android app.

Smart matches

People who teach what you want and want what you teach, ranked by how well you fit.

Time bank credits

Teach an hour, earn a credit. Spend it on any lesson. Five welcome credits to start.

Trust built in

A QR check-in proves every lesson, reviews after every swap, a Trusted badge at 4.5 stars.

Chat and workspace

Talk with your partner, set a goal, tick off milestones and keep shared notes.

Skill Galaxy

The whole community as a live solar system: see who swaps with whom.

Badges and leaders

Eight badges from real lessons, and the top teachers of the week on a podium.
